# Sample report (abridged)

An abridged report to show the format and honesty labels. **All figures are illustrative** — in a real run, every verified number comes from the live listing, the current fee schedule and real supplier quotes.

**Request:** "The team picked a wall-mounted jersey display case. Target price $45, Sports & Outdoors > Fan Shop. Three competitor links and two supplier quotes attached."

---

## Section 1 · Product definition
A wall-mounted acrylic case (~32 × 24 × 3 in) for a single sports jersey, UV-filtering front panel, includes hanger and wall hardware. Buyers: fans and collectors displaying a signed or favorite jersey; gift buyers around playoffs and holidays. Two of three competitor links match; the third is a tabletop case and is excluded.

## Section 2 · Hard stops
Price $45 ✅ · Amazon US ✅ · no special regulation ✅ · **IP:** generic case, no team logos ✅ (team-branded versions would be a hard stop without a license).

## Section 8 · Verdict: ❌ DON'T

**Bulky, fragile and thin-margin: the shipping profile eats the markup.**

| Dimension | Rating | One line |
|---|---|---|
| Market demand | ⚠️ | Steady but seasonal peaks around playoffs/holidays |
| Competition | ⚠️ | Leaders have 1,000+ reviews; few angles left |
| Unit economics | ❌ | ~7% margin before confirming the oversize fee |
| Execution | ❌ | Oversize + breakage + 500 MOQ won't fit a $3K test |

**Three fatal reasons:** oversize fulfillment fees; breakage returns on acrylic; MOQ too high for a small first batch.

**Alternatives worth a look:** a smaller wall-display product (for caps or medals, well under oversize thresholds), or a flat-packed version that ships compactly — run each through the same process.
